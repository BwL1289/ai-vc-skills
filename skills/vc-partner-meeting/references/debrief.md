# The debrief

The vc-partner-meeting skill's debrief, written at its step 7. The skill's own "The debrief" section links here and
keeps the rules that bind every part of it; the skill's test checks both.

For each question asked, in the drill's order, then, in one-message mode, each other pasted answer (step 6):

- **The question,** its lens (or "your question" for one only the founder asked), and the founder's line it came
  from (or, for a mismatch or flag, the tool's row), quoted.
- **The founder's answer,** quoted with its label.
- **What was weak,** only where something was: it did not answer the question asked; it gave no figure where the
  question asked for one; a figure had no definition or period; it disagrees with another line of the material or with a
  tool result (both quoted, the reason left as a bracketed ask such as `[what the deck's 24 months assumes]`, never an
  argument about which assumptions would make the stated figure true); it claims less than the material's own line on
  the same point (both quoted); it uses a phrase the vc-positioning skill flags; it ran long before the point. Say "held
  up" when nothing was weak.
- **A stronger answer,** at most four sentences, the point first, built only from the founder's material and tool
  results, each fact traceable to a label. Never stronger than the facts: no number, customer, investor, partner,
  credential or plan that is not in the material. Each gap is a bracketed ask. When the spoken answer claims less than
  the material's own line, the stronger answer is tiered within the same four sentences: it leads with the strongest
  items a partner can check, each with its label, then says the rest plainly for what it is (a plan, an early result, a
  figure not yet measured), never dressed up as more. Where the answer gives a different figure, count or date than the
  line, or takes the claim back, it is a disagreement, as above, and the stronger answer never restores the bigger
  claim: its bracketed ask asks which stands.
- **One next step,** for a standing question only ([standing-prep.md](standing-prep.md)): where the material says
  nothing on it, its bracketed ask carries it; where the founder's answer is short, it gets the one specific next step
  that file names for it, such as putting spoken interest in writing, lining up references before the meetings, or
  having today's runway in months ready from `equity_runway_plan`. A next step never assumes an answer the founder did
  not give. It is written once, in the answer's Standing prep, not again in this question's debrief.

Then **what a yes would need:** the evidence that would answer each kept question still open after the drill, as a
list of bracketed asks, ordered by tier. It is a list of things to show, not a prediction: never say a partner or a
firm would invest, pass or score the company.

When the founder changes a figure after an answer was drafted or debriefed, "A figure that changes" in
[drill.md](drill.md) applies, in either mode.
