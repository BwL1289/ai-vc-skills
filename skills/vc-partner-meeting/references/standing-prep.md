# Standing prep questions

The vc-partner-meeting skill's standing prep questions: a partner is likely to ask or check these whatever the pitch
says. The skill's own "Standing prep questions" section links here and says how they are used; each question is also a
standing row of the question bank, word for word, and the skill's test checks both.

## The questions

| Question a partner is likely to ask or check                  | What the founder should have ready                                                      | One next step after a short answer                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Which other investors are you meeting, and who has committed? | A true answer at the level of detail the founder chooses; never an implied interest     | Ask each investor whose interest or yes is only spoken so far to put it in writing, and cite only what is written |
| Will your existing investors take part in this round?         | Their answer as the founder has it, or the plan to ask them before the meeting          | Ask them before the meeting, and have their answer, and any amount, as they gave it                               |
| Who has passed, and why?                                      | The reasons given, and what the founder changed or would answer                         | Write down each reason as it was given, with what changed since or the answer to it                               |
| Whom will the partner call, on your list and off it?          | Customers, former colleagues and investors who may be called; brief the ones you list   | Line up the references, each one's agreement first, and brief them before the meetings start                      |
| What happens if this round does not close?                    | Runway on today's cash from `equity_runway_plan` with no raise, what gets cut, the plan | Have today's runway in months ready, from `equity_runway_plan` on today's cash, spend and revenue                 |
| What happens after this meeting, and when?                    | A question for the founder to ask the partner, not to guess                             | Ask it before the meeting ends, and write down the step and its date                                              |

Where the material says nothing on a standing question, its bracketed ask carries it. Where the founder's answer is
short, it gets the one next step in the last column, the one that gets the answer ready before the meeting, written
once, in the answer's Standing prep; a next step never assumes an answer the founder did not give. An answer is short
when it does not yet give what the middle column says the founder should have ready, whether it came with the material
or in the drill; a brief answer that gives it gets no next step. The sixth question's step always shows, since it is
the founder's to ask.

The first five topics came, as ideas, from the investor-persona prompts the skill's Sources credit, which cite no
source for them; the wording, the sixth question and the list as a whole are the skill's own. The next steps are the
skill's own, added after its first live drill. All six questions and their next steps are unverified.
