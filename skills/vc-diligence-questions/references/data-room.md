# Data room and diligence questions

This file holds the vc-diligence-questions skill's data-room checklist and its own diligence questions about the
company's legal footing and its cap table's records. Its rows are this skill's own and unverified; the questions a
partner is likely to ask in a meeting live in the vc-partner-meeting skill's question bank, which this skill reads in
place.

Rules for every row: a question or a document, never an answer; no figure, benchmark or bar; no person or firm
named; IDs unique and never the same as a bank ID.

## The checklist

"From" is the first stage at which a partner is likely to ask for the document: pre-seed, seed, Series A or later.
